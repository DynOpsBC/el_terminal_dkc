# BCWMSApp Android

BCWMSApp Mobile gives warehouse operators a scan-first Business Central execution experience for receiving, put-away, picking, shipping, movement, count, production consumption, production output, and assembly workflows.

The app supports camera barcode scanning, device registration, secure Microsoft Entra authentication, offline-first local storage, sync conflict review, and warehouse task execution against the BCWMSApp Business Central APIs.
